# Income collection: definitions and handoff (6 November 2026)

## Data and reproducibility

Run `uv run python studies/cesena/prepare_income_evidence.py` from the project root. It reads the frozen ZIPs in `data/raw/cesena_income_baseline/`, not the mutable download cache, and writes `data/processed/cesena/income_followup/income_components.parquet` (1,768 rows: 13 cities × 17 years × 8 components) and `income_denominators.csv` (221 city-years). These paths are ignored, so retain the baseline snapshots separately before deleting local data. The MEF files were fetched again on 6 November: all 17 archive hashes match the frozen files, as recorded in `release_check.json`; no MEF revision was identified at the level of archive bytes. A direct check of the conventional 2025 ZIP URL returned HTTP 404: this does not prove that no newer release exists elsewhere.

The panel includes canonical ISTAT and stable cadastral codes. The source inventory covers every cached year's income and taxpayer headers. The component table retains separate amount/count source labels and status. `blank_or_unparsed` is deliberately broad; a blank may be suppressed, missing or another source condition and is not zero. `absent_schema` means no matching source column. Amounts are nominal euros, not thousands. Frequencies overlap across components and are not a partition of people. Self-employment, business and participation frequencies include zero values, as stated by the source headers. 'Business' in the municipal table is income attributable to the entrepreneur after specified shares and losses, not total enterprise revenue or profit.

The 2012 archive abbreviates both business labels to `Reddito spettanza imprenditore ...`; the parser recognizes those and the longer labels. All seven components have nonmissing amounts in each of the 221 rows. The reported total-income amount and frequency are present only in 2023–2024 (26 of 221 rows), not in the earlier municipal schemas. This is availability of a separate column, not evidence of no earlier income. Compare the distinct source labels across all years before treating definitions as invariant.

The headline mean is the sum of available positive-bracket amounts divided by their frequencies (`bracket_gini_and_median`), in constant 2020 euros. All 221 study city-years have all positive brackets after reconstruction; Faenza and Imola in 2023 and 2024 each have a reconstructed top bracket. Their nonpositive bracket is also missing, so the total-minus-known-cells residual includes any suppressed nonpositive values; it is an approximation. In Cesena 2024 there were 77,117 reported taxpayers, 75,340 total-income frequency and 75,335 positive-bracket taxpayers. The corresponding positive-bracket amount (EUR 2,067,171,292) differs from the reported total (EUR 2,067,149,907), so neither denominator nor numerator may be interchanged without disclosure. These counts and sums are in `income_denominators.csv` for all years.

## Deflator audit — must resolve before interpreting the headline

The frozen processed panel was reproduced with `italy_deflator`: the local panel contains annual Italian HICP rates through 2025, stored to one decimal. The function chains those rates and anchors the level at 2020. The frozen panel and claim outputs have been copied alongside the source ZIPs. `deflator_audit.csv` compares that chain to the Eurostat HICP all-items published annual-average index, retrieved 6 November 2026 (2015=100; 2008 90.4, 2020 103.0, 2024 122.3). The frozen calculation's 2024/2008 price ratio is 1.34872065 versus 1.35287611 in the retrieved index. With the same Cesena nominal means, the real change is +4.76097% versus +4.43919%, respectively. Rounded rate chaining and source-vintage revisions are possible contributors; their individual effects have not been isolated. The direct-index comparison is a diagnostic, not an authorized replacement of the baseline, and no conclusions or figures were rewritten.

Source: [Eurostat annual HICP dataset](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-datasets/-/prc_hicp_aind); [API query](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/prc_hicp_aind?geo=IT&coicop=CP00&unit=INX_A_AVG&sinceTimePeriod=2008&untilTimePeriod=2024). Raw JSON is in the frozen folder. The fallback linked NIC/HICP path was not used in the reproduced current panel calculation. A future correction must examine the vintage, series changes, exact annual averages and all consumers of the shared deflator before replacing it.

## Definitions and outstanding checks

[MEF's tax-year 2024 definitions](https://www1.finanze.gov.it/finanze/analisi_stat/public/v_4_0_0/contenuti/definizione_variabili_2024_irpef.pdf) say the broader `reddito complessivo` includes income subject to cedolare secca; its total *net of* cedolare secca excludes income subject to substitute taxes. The ordinary flat-rate income treatment and each municipal historical definition still need a vintage-by-vintage comparison; do not assume every substitute-tax component is included or excluded uniformly. Taxpayers can include people declaring only substitute-tax income. The same MEF definitions state that simplified-accounting business income moved to cash accounting in 2017 and loss rules changed in 2018. These are relevant breaks, not evidence of economic change.

[MEF methodology](https://www1.finanze.gov.it/finanze/analisi_stat/public/v_4_0_0/contenuti/nota_metodologica_2019.pdf) describes municipal figures in euros, privacy suppression, and fiscal domicile on 31 December of the declaration year (usually but not always registry residence). Regional metadata describe evasion, exempt income and income taxed at source as outside the declarations. Employment-and-assimilated income includes more than wages; it does not measure hours, full-time equivalent pay, or changes for the same individuals.

An independent presentation of the same MEF source is the [regional configurable income table](https://statistica.regione.emilia-romagna.it/dati/statistica-self-service/dichiarazioni_redditi); a matched Cesena/Faenza/Imola numerical cross-check is pending. It is a cross-check on extraction, not an independent income source. Municipal separately taxed-income coverage by year is unresolved. Consult the original labels and the MEF definitions before adding components or using residuals; their sum is not established to exhaust the headline total.
