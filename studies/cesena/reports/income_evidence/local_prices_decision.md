# Local-price collection decision — 6 November 2026

The [ISTAT annual NIC dataflow](https://esploradati.istat.it/SDMXWS/rest/data/IT1,167_742_DF_DCSP_NIC2B2015_1,1.0/A.IT+ITD51+ITD52+ITD53+ITD54+ITD55+ITD56+ITD57+ITD58+ITD59+040007+040012+099014+039014+037032+036005+035033+038008+036023+034027+033032+039010+037006.40.4.00) returned all-items annual-average index numbers (2015=100) for Italy and the nine provinces covering the 13 cities for 2016–2025. The raw CSV is `data/raw/cesena_income_baseline/istat_nic_2016_2025.csv`. The dimension key is annual / area / NIC annual mean / index number / all items. City codes were included in the request and returned no city observations; this dataflow returning no city series does not prove that no other source exists. `local_prices_coverage.csv` maps each peer to its province and distinguishes endpoint coverage. Values for 2018 and 2024 exist for all nine province codes and Italy, but 2008 is not in this dataflow. Do not splice other bases without official linking coefficients and source metadata.

The [Romagna Chamber of Commerce](https://servizi.romagna.camcom.it/informazione-economico-statistica/osservatorio-economico-e-sociale/prezzi-al-consumo-nel-2021-nelle-province-di-forli-cesena-e-di-rimini/index.htm?ID_D=11445) identifies the Forlì-Cesena measure it describes as the index for Forlì extended to the province; it is not an observation in Cesena. Its [2024 report](https://www.romagna.camcom.it/attachments/file/view?canCache=0&hash=1ba18cba75caafa6c8d07925908ba5ed672b3626f95d0727791fafef3177a431) explicitly says provincial data are not comparable to Italy. Clarification of that caveat and sampling geography for every other province is needed before any geographic-vs-national index calculation is presented. Some peers share the same provincial proxy (Cesena and Forlì, Faenza and Ravenna, Carpi and Modena, Imola and Bologna); this cannot measure differences within those pairs.

The [regional source index](https://statistica.regione.emilia-romagna.it/statistiche-per-temi/prezzi/dati/tabelle-prezzi-consumo) links province series from 2016; its old I.Stat link currently redirects to a retirement notice. The retrieved IstatData API is a working replacement, not evidence of municipal series. [ISTAT methodology](https://www.istat.it/informazioni-sulla-rilevazione/prezzi-territoriale/) explains annual averages and multiple sources of price observations. No full-city 2008–2024 index panel was established. Preserve the full-peer national-adjusted comparison; a provincial proxy sensitivity is provisional until its comparability caveat is resolved, and must be labelled as a proxy. Spatial price levels are a different question.
