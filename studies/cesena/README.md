- **Pitfalls specific to this study:**
  - **2016 `D.Lgs. 118/2011` municipal accounting harmonization break:** Italian municipal balance sheets changed accounting structure in 2016 (`D.P.R. 194/1996` Funzioni/Servizi in `2008–2015` vs `D.Lgs. 118/2011` Missioni/Programmi in `2016+`, introducing FPV and FCDE). Rows in `2008–2015` carry `regime = "dpr194"` and `flag = "break_dlgs118"`; never splice levels or compute growth rates across the `2015 -> 2016` boundary.
  - **OpenBilanci parent Mission JSON `NaN` propagation:** In `openbilanci.it/armonizzati/bilanci/<slug>/spese/dettaglio.json`, when any child Program of a Mission has an empty/zero capital or current cell (`nan` in JSON), OpenBilanci's backend propagates `nan` to the parent Mission's `composition.investimenti_value` (e.g., Cesena 2021 Mission 05 and Mission 11). Always sum `correnti_value`, `investimenti_value`, `incremento_fin_value`, and `altro_value` across the `children` Program nodes (`bdap._mission_composition`).
  - **Finanza Locale `CCOX Quadro 10` multiple `'TOTALE'` rows & `Quadro 09` 2016 label change (`2016–2018`):** In `CCOX Quadro 10`, the HTML page stacks three tables (`IMPEGNI`, `PAGAMENTI IN C/COMPETENZA`, `PAGAMENTI IN C/RESIDUI`), each ending with `'TOTALE'`; always take the first `'TOTALE'` row (`IMPEGNI`), never the last (`PAGAMENTI IN C/RESIDUI`). In `CCOX Quadro 09`, the 2016 IMU row is labeled `'Imposta municipale propria'` (without `'(IMU)'`, which appears in `2017–2018`), and waste tax in `2016–2018` is booked across `'Tributo comunale sui rifiuti e sui servizi'` and `'Tassa per la raccolta e lo smaltimento dei rifiuti solidi urbani (TARSU)'`.
  - **OpenBilanci `storico` ZIP 404 for Reggio nell'Emilia (`035033`):** Because of the apostrophe in the slug, `storico.openbilanci.it` returns HTTP 404 for Reggio nell'Emilia's batch ZIP; `bdap.py` falls back automatically to `finanzalocale.interno.gov.it` `CCOU` (`Quadri 02, 03, 04`) for `2008–2014`.
  - **MEF municipal IRPEF CSV trailing semicolon (`2019–2022`) & `2014` bracket wording:** Data rows in `2019–2022` end with an extra `;` not present in the header line; read the header column count `n_cols` from line 0 and pass `usecols=range(n_cols)` to `pd.read_csv`. In `2014`, the zero/negative bracket is labeled `minore di zero euro` instead of `minore o uguale a zero euro`.
  - **MEF blank top bracket (2023 onward):** the `>120k` cell is blank whenever the `<=0` cell is blank, for about half of the municipalities with 10k+ taxpayers (apparently complementary suppression). Reading a blank as no income removes about 10% of their income. Recover it from `Reddito complessivo` totals (`mef_comuni.parse_brackets`), which exist only from 2023.
  - **ISTAT migration codes:** `REGM`/`DEREGM` are moves to and from other Italian municipalities (`INTNETMIGR_REGOF`); `REGOTHC`/`DEREGOTHC` are moves to and from abroad (`INTERTNMIG_REGOF`). Population change also includes `STAT_ADJUST`, large in census years (`istat_demo.add_balance_components`).
  - **ASIA single-unit breaks:** one local unit can move a municipal sector by thousands in a year (Cesena food manufacturing 2022, Forlì retail 2019). Run `economy.employment_anomalies` and report results with `employment_sensitivity`. Agency staff are counted at the agency. The ISTAT flow answers only to NUTS 2010 codes for provinces (`ITD58`, not `ITH58`: HTTP 404); `0010` (total) and `10` (food) collide if the ATECO column is read as numbers (`load_areas` reads strings). R1 (closed): the food dip is local to Forlì-Cesena and sits in the 250+ class (`economy.sector_size_swing`); cause unknown.
  - **OMI zero rents:** a zone without a rental quote is filed as 0; treat it as missing. Up to about half of the rows in some cities have no rental quote.
  - **SIOPE:** comune-to-Unione transfers (`U1040102005`, `U2030102005`) and Unione-to-comune transfers (`U1040102003`, `U2030102003`) must be netted when consolidating. The 2016 files use old codes, so macroaggregates are missing there, not zero.
  - **SIOPE chart of accounts and revenue (added 2026-11):** the 2016 files use the old codes, where Titolo 3 is the repayment of loans; from 2017 Titolo 3 is the purchase of financial assets (shares, deposits) and the repayment is Titolo 4 (`U4`). The panel read `U3` as repayment for 2017–2024 until this was fixed (fields `rimborso_prestiti_cassa_eur`, `spese_totali_cassa_eur`, `consolidated_totali_*`; no finding used them). Revenue lines (IMU `E1010106`, ICI `E1010108`, addizionale `E1010116`, TARSU/TARI `E1010151` and `E1010161`) exist only in the 10-digit codes of 2017 on. The waste tax is booked in the municipal budget in some cities and collected through the operator's tariff in others (Carpi, Ferrara and Forlì show almost none; Cesena fell from €15.4M to €2.6M in 2023), so any ratio with current revenue is also shown without it (`rigidity_cash_ex_tari_pct`). The cash rigidity proxy tracks the official accrual ratio on the 2017–2018 overlap (correlation 0.97) but is never joined to it.
  - **PNRR tender file:** it names no contracting authority or location; a name match attributes national utility lots to whichever city they mention.
  - **Narrative numbers:** never type a figure in `findings.md`. Add it to `analysis/claims.py` and tag it; the hand-typed `+27.9%` and `1,070.1` matched no correct result.
