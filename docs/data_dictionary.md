# Data dictionary

`data/processed/panel.parquet` (long format; built by `uv run python -m cifresignificative.core.clean.build`).

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| iso3 | ISO3 country code; `EU27` = EU27_2020 aggregate |
| geo | source geo code (Eurostat style: `EL` = Greece, `UK`, `EU27_2020`) |
| year | calendar year (annual series) |
| indicator | name from `src/cifresignificative/core/catalog.py` (Eurostat) or `wb_*` (World Bank, `sources/worldbank.py`) |
| value | numeric value, unit per catalog filters/notes |
| flag | Eurostat status flag (p provisional, e estimate, b break, ...) or null |
| theme | output, investment, fiscal, demography, labour, education, pensions, inequality, prices, external |
| source | `eurostat:<dataset>` / `worldbank:<code>` |

Reproduce: `uv run python -m cifresignificative.core.fetch && uv run python -m cifresignificative.core.clean.build` (raw files cached in `data/raw/`, provenance in `data/raw/manifest.json`; `--force` re-downloads).

## Other tables
- `data/processed/regional.parquet`: NUTS2 regions of EU27 countries (`geo`, `country`, `year`, `indicator`, `value`, `flag`): GDP per head (PPS, EUR per inhabitant), unemployment, tertiary attainment 25-64, female employment 20-64, population, plus (added for the regional screen) employment by sex and 55-64, youth and long-term unemployment, NEET, early leavers, R&D spending and personnel, investment and compensation totals, broadband, fertility, life expectancy, old-age dependency, median age, net migration, physicians, AROPE. `ZZ` extra-regio codes are dropped; coverage differs by indicator (AROPE 2015+, broadband to 2021, physicians to 2020, 137-284 regions). Region codes change between NUTS vintages (303 codes in the file, 242 in NUTS 2021): `regional_nuts2021.parquet` has them translated, see below.
- `data/processed/pensions_oecd_2024.parquet`: OECD Pensions at a Glance 2024 cross-section (gross/net replacement rates at 50/100/200% of average earnings, normal retirement ages for current and future entrants, pension wealth, employment by age) with `SEX`/`AGE`/`OPTIONALITY` kept; not a time series.

- `data/processed/sectors.parquet`: Eurostat national accounts by industry (10 NACE sections, A*10-style): `gva_clv20_meur` (real, chain-linked 2020 EUR m), `gva_cp_meur` (nominal), `hours_ths` (hours worked, thousands). Real euro levels are not comparable across countries without PPPs: compare growth. Sector L (real estate) is excluded in the analysis because of imputed rents. Coverage (download of 25 October 2026, Eurostat release of 21 October): all 27 members with all nine analysis sections to 2024; 2025 is a first estimate for 25 members (Bulgaria and Latvia have no 2025 value added), and past years are revised at each release (up to 31% in single cells). `shift_share` uses only the two compared years: it once dropped any sector missing in another year, which left five members out (the check is `tests/studies/italy_productivity/test_sectors.py`).

- `data/processed/spending.parquet`: general-government spending (`kind` = `cofog` by function incl. sub-functions such as old age GF1002, debt interest GF0107; or `nature` by economic item such as D1PAY wages, D62_D632PAY social benefits, D9PAY capital transfers, D92PAY investment grants and D99PAY other capital transfers (the last two added 2026-10-18); `unit` PC_GDP, PC_TOT or, for cofog only, MIO_EUR = million euro at current prices; euro levels are for reading shares in absolute terms, compare growth or shares across countries, not levels). 2021-23 contain large one-off capital transfers for Italy.

- `data/processed/highered.parquet`: higher-education detail from Eurostat UOE tables (`table` = he_grads_field, he_students_field, he_grads_age, he_pop_age; columns `isced11` level (ED5-8 all tertiary, ED5 short-cycle, ED6 bachelor, ED7 master, ED8 doctoral), `iscedf13` field, `sex`, `age`). Derived rates in the panel (STEM share, women's share, doctorates per 1000, mean graduation age) are computed in `clean/build.py`: STEM = ISCED-F 05+06+07; graduate counts are degrees not people.

- `data/processed/mef_irpef.parquet`: MEF Department of Finance IRPEF return statistics by income class (long table: `filing_year`, `tax_year` = filing year - 1, `code`, `group` TOT/DIP/PEN/TIT/IMP/PRO/AGR, `class_label`, `class_lo`/`class_hi` in euro, `variable` (normalised) / `variable_raw`, `measure` freq / amount_keur / mean_keur / pct, `value`, `suppressed`, `jump`). Source files `data/external/source/mef_irpef/` (238 non-empty CSV of 315 requested; `mapping.csv`, `download.sh` kept). Tax years 2008-2024 for the distribution tables (`01xx`), 2011-2024 for VAT-number holders, 2012-2024 for employees/pensioners/all (no 2015 employee and pensioner detail: the 2016 files are empty on the server). Amounts and means in thousand euro. Classes are by **reddito complessivo**, which excludes income taxed under the flat-rate regimes (forfetario, vantaggio, minimi): flat-rate filers sit mostly in the zero class. Classes below zero: tax year 2017 anomaly (-10 bn business loss in the lowest class) and a collapse from about 120,000 to about 3,000 taxpayers from 2018. `***` = suppressed (3.4% of cells). Variable names drift (renames are not merged; `mef_irpef.variable_spans` lists first/last year). Used by notebook 32.
- `data/processed/mef_irpef_dims.parquet`: the same MEF IRPEF returns by other classifications (`dimension` = region, activity (ATECO section), age, turnover (VAT turnover class), ventile (twentile of reddito complessivo)); same columns as `mef_irpef.parquet` plus `dimension`, `class_order` and `topic` (the file's "Tematica"). Model `AE` (individuals by prevalent income, turnover classes only, filing years 2012-2025, 84 files, tax year 2022 empty on the server for the professionals' RE table): groups `PIMP` (entrepreneurs, form sections RF and RG, plus the variants for total income of at least EUR 100,000) and `PPRO` (regular self-employed professionals, section RE: `Totale compensi`, `Totale spese`, `Differenza`); table codes changed in 2014 (`AETIPRE0406` to `AETPREA0406`), the ≥100,000 variants are told apart by `topic`; the files end with a second `TOTALE` row for items without a class split, merged by the loader. Raw files `data/external/source/mef_irpef_<name>/` (688 CSV: 604 by the crawler plus the 84 AE files in `mef_irpef_fatturato/`, downloaded with `scripts/fetch_mef_irpef.py`, `catalog.csv` per folder). Table codes repeat across classifications: key by (`dimension`, `code`). Tax years 2011/2012-2024; the 2016 employee and pensioner regional tables are empty on the server. Group `MIN` = flat-rate filers (minimi, regime di vantaggio, regimi agevolati; sector and region tables only). Tax year 2024 uses the ATECO 2025 section list. Quirks fixed in the loader: NACE names containing `;`, Trentino and Alto Adige in two rows with one code, a twentile table with a bound column and no taxpayer count, the first twentile holding zero and (to 2017) negative incomes. Used by notebook 33.
- `data/processed/selfemp.parquet`: self-employment long table (`table`, `wstatus`, `sex`, `age`, `nace_r2`, `iso3`, `year`, `value`, `flag`): `selfemp_share_status` (% of employment 15-64: self-employed, employers `SELF_S`, own-account `SELF_NS`; sex T/M/F; 2005-2025), `selfemp_share_age` (% of employment by age band 15-24, 25-49, 50-64), `selfemp_nace_ths` and `employed_nace_ths` (thousands by NACE section, 2008-2025; Eurostat leaves out small cells, so the self-employed table has gaps), `arop_by_status` (at-risk-of-poverty rate, 60% of median, age 18-64, by most frequent activity status in the previous year; `NSAL` = employed except employees = self-employed plus contributing family workers; 2009-2025). 27 EU members plus GBR and others; many `b` (break) flags, dropped in the analysis; kept out of the annual panel on purpose, no glossary rows. Income of the self-employed is not available from Eurostat.
- `data/processed/size_class.parquet`: Eurostat structural business statistics by employment size class, one row per (`iso3`, `year`, `vintage`, `nace_r2`, `size_emp`) with `enterprises`, `persons`, `va_meur`, `pli` (GDP price level index, EU27 = 100, `prc_ppp_ind`) and `flag`. `vintage` `old` = `sbs_sc_sca_r2` (2005-2020, value added at factor cost, economy = `B-N_S95_X_K`), `new` = `sbs_sc_ovw` (2021 onwards, basic prices, economy = `B-S_X_O_S94`, wider scope): never combine them. Classes 0-9, 10-19, 20-49, 50-249, `GE250`, `TOTAL`; sections B-N without K. Coverage: five classes complete for the economy aggregate for 13-23 members a year (2019: 23, without AT, CY, DK, PT) and 25-27 in 2021-24; Italy has 2013, 2015, 2016, 2019, 2020 and 2021-24 (2017 flagged `b`, 2018 missing); sums of sections are not built (small sections have confidential large-firm cells, 8-12 countries a year); empty classes (0 persons) occur in small sections. Kept out of the annual panel on purpose, no glossary rows.
- `data/processed/income_dist.parquet`: EU-SILC income distribution, long table (`table`, `quant_inc`, `iso3`, `year`, `value`, `flag`): `income_share` (quintiles QU1-QU5, bottom and top decile D1, D10; % of equivalised disposable income), `s80_s20`, `gini_disp` (= panel `gini`, from 2014), `gini_pre_pens_incl` and `gini_pre_pens_excl` (Gini before social transfers with pensions counted as transfers / as market income). 2005-2025, 27 EU members (24 from 2005, the rest from 2006-2010) plus GBR and a few others; `year` is the survey year, incomes refer mostly to the year before; flags `b` (break) and `p` are dropped in the analysis. Kept out of the annual panel on purpose (more correlated inequality series would feed the national screens); no glossary rows needed.
- `data/processed/sectors64.parquet`: A*64 industries (62 NACE groups): real/nominal gross value added and hours worked, same units as `sectors.parquet`. Coverage limit: hours worked at 2-digit level are missing for Germany, France, Belgium, Portugal, Bulgaria, Latvia, Lithuania and Estonia (15 industries or fewer), so the industry panel (notebook 28) has about 19 countries. In the October 2026 release 2025 exists at this detail for few industries (Italy 15 of 57): notebook 13 stops at 2024.
- `data/processed/earnings_cases.parquet`: annual earnings by household case (`ecase`: single at 50/100/167% of the average wage, single parent with 2 children at 67%, couple with 2 children at 100%) and component (`estruct`: GRS gross, TAX, SOC social contributions, FAM family benefits, NET), in EUR. Break in 2024 (new basis of the average wage, flag `b`): compare rates within a year, not levels across 2023/2024.
- `data/processed/projections_eurostat.parquet`: EUROPOP2023 demographic projections by scenario (BSL baseline, LFRT low fertility, HMIGR high migration, NMIGR no migration), 2024-2100.
- `data/processed/ageing_report.parquet`: EC/EPC 2024 Ageing Report projections 2022-2070 per country (`sheet` a = demography/macro, b = pensions, c = health/long-term care/education/total cost of ageing; `section` and `variable` as in the annex).
- `data/processed/long_history.parquet`: AMECO 1960-2025 in national currency and native units (real GDP, population, employment, hours, debt, investment, unemployment, wage share) plus derived ratios; compare countries through ratios or indices only.
- `data/processed/istat_irregular_work.parquet`: Istat irregular-employment rates by industry (Italy only), latest edition per year. Employment-status code 9 is treated as the total (inferred).
- `data/processed/vintages/`: dated snapshots (`panel_`, `regional_`, `regional_extra_`, `highfreq_`; last 12 of each, written only when the values change); `uv run python scripts/diff_vintages.py [table|all]` lists revisions, additions and drops between the two latest snapshots of a table.

- `data/processed/migration.parquet`: immigration and emigration by citizenship (`TOTAL`, `NAT` = nationals) and age group, plus population by citizenship (Eurostat migr_imm1ctz, migr_emi1ctz, migr_pop1ctz). Derived per-capita rates are in the panel (`immigration_per_1000`, `emigration_nationals_25_34_per_1000`, `net_migration_nationals_25_34_per_1000`, `foreign_citizens_share_pct`, ...).

- `data/external/italy_governments.csv`: Italian governments since 1946 (name, prime minister, party, composition, legislature, president, start, end = day the next government took office; the current government has an empty end). Built by `scripts/build_italy_governments.py` from the English Wikipedia list, cross-checked against the Italian edition (all governments since 2001 agree on dates).
- `data/external/italy_policies.csv`: 46 laws, decrees and EU/ECB decisions with date, type of date (enacted, in force, announced), reference, what changed, indicators likely affected, an `evidence` label (`verified`: confirmed in a source during the project; `partly verified`: law/number confirmed, exact day from memory; `unverified`: not confirmed) and source URLs. Curated by hand: extend it row by row and keep the label honest. Timing only: it says nothing about causal effects.

- `data/processed/italy_tax_gap_mef.parquet`: Italy's tax-gap estimates by tax, 2018-2022 (EUR million, upper estimate) and propensity to evade (% of tax due), from tables I.1, I.4 and I.5 of the Ministry of Economy and Finance's 2025 report on evasion (`sources/mef_gap.py`; the PDF is in `data/external/source/evasion/`). The ministry's method differs from the Commission's VAT-gap series.
- `data/processed/euklems_growth.parquet`: EU KLEMS & INTANProd growth accounts by industry, 1995-2021: labour productivity per hour growth (`LP1_G`), contributions of TFP, labour composition, ICT and other tangible capital and intangibles (`LP1Con*`, pp) and the TFP index. Built only if the large raw CSV (`data/external/source/euklems/growth-accounts-extended.csv`, ~300 MB, gitignored) was downloaded with `cifresignificative.core.fetch`.

- `data/processed/italy_bready_cities.parquet`: World Bank Subnational Business Ready 2025, 13 Italian cities, five topics (business entry, location, utility services, dispute resolution, insolvency), topic and pillar total scores 0-100. The data file must be downloaded with a browser (openknowledge.worldbank.org is a JavaScript app): `cifresignificative.core.fetch` does this with the headless browser.
- `data/processed/italy_unobserved_economy.parquet` and `italy_underground_by_industry.parquet`: Istat "Economia non osservata nei conti nazionali" (2025 release, 2011-2023): underground (under-reporting, irregular labour, other) and illegal activities, EUR million and % of GDP; underground economy as % of industry value added. Manual download (see docs/manual_data.md); the file is `data/external/source/istat_unobserved/Tavole-Economia-non-osservata_2025.xlsx`.
- `data/external/glossary.csv`: plain-language name, unit and definition (English and Italian) of every panel indicator and of the main concepts (PPS, median of the others, z-gap, COFOG...). Read by `cifresignificative.core.glossary` and shown in the dashboard; a test fails if a new indicator has no entry, so add a row when you add an indicator.
- `data/processed/esif_absorption.parquet`: EU cohesion funds 2014-2020 by member state and fund: programmed amount and total eligible expenditure declared at the latest period (Cohesion Open Data Platform, dataset 99js-gm52). Absorption above 100% reflects over-commitment of programmes.

- `data/processed/regional_extra.parquet`: EQI (`nuts2_eqi`, `_quality`, `_impartiality`, `_corruption`, `_margin_of_error`; waves 2010, 2013, 2017, 2021, 2024; 186 NUTS2 regions, Germany and Belgium absent because they are published at NUTS1; standardised within each wave, so compare ranks, not levels across waves) and structural business statistics per NUTS2 (`nuts2_sbs_manufacturing_employment_share_pct`, `nuts2_sbs_local_units_per_1000_inhabitants`, `nuts2_sbs_wages_per_employee_keur`; 2008-2020; sections B-N without K, kept only where all 12 sections are published; flag = union of the components' flag characters). Same long format as `regional.parquet`, built by `build.build_regional_extra`.
- `data/processed/regional_nuts2021.parquet` and `regional_extra_nuts2021.parquet`: the two tables above on the NUTS 2021 geography (`clean/nuts.py`), with `geo_old` (original code) and `geo_method` (`native` or `renamed`). Renamed history comes from the GISCO area-overlap crosswalk (France 2013, Greece 2010, Slovenia 2010, Poland 2013 and NUTS 2006 codes); splits (FI18, HR04, HU10, IE01, IE02, LT00, PL12) and codes that exist only from 2024 (NL35, NL36, PT19, PT1A-D) are left out, extra-regio codes are dropped. Built only when `data/raw/gisco_nuts/` exists. Vintage snapshots are kept for `regional_nuts2021`.
- `data/processed/italy_internal_migration.parquet`: Istat registry changes of residence between Italian regions (dataflow 28_185_DF_DCIS_MIGRAZIONI_1): `origin`, `destination` (NUTS2, translated from Istat's NUTS 2010 codes ITD/ITE to ITH/ITI), `year` 2002-2025, `sex`, `citizenship`, `value` (moves), `provisional`. Age is a total only. Macro-area aggregates dropped; moves within a region are in the file and excluded from net flows.
- `data/processed/italy_cpt_spending.parquet`: Conti Pubblici Territoriali, extended public sector, expenditure by region (NUTS2, Trento and Bolzano separate), sector of intervention (29), level of government and account (`total`, `capital`, `current`), 2000-2023, EUR million nominal. Attributed to where the spending is incurred (includes pensions and central spending). The source files sit at hashed URLs found from the catalog page by `sources/cpt.py`.
- `data/raw/trasparenza/<site>/` (not a table): files scraped from a municipal Amministrazione trasparente portal by `core/sources/trasparenza.py` (`catalog.json`, `lists/`, `details/`, `files/<doc>/<att>_<name>`, manifest `files.csv` with sha256). The Rendiconto approval acts carry CSVs in `;` separated Latin-1 with Italian decimals (`1.234,56` in the old layout, `1234,56` in the new one); the expenditure file has Impegnato/FPV/Pagato per mission, the last three columns being the totals. Signed `.p7m` envelopes are skipped unless asked.
- Cesena study, `data/processed/cesena/rendiconto_accrual.parquet`: one city only, one row per year 2018 onwards from the Rendiconto CSVs (2022 onwards from the portal; 2018-2021 from the old site `sititematici`, `core/sources/comune_archive.py`, whose zero-padded layout with macroaggregate rows is read by `archive_row`) (`entrate_correnti_acc_eur`/`_risc_eur` = Titoli 1-3 Tipologie accrued/collected; `spesa_personale_imp_eur`, `interessi_passivi_imp_eur`, `rimborso_prestiti_imp_eur`, `spese_correnti_imp_eur` = committed; `rigidity_accrual_pct`). A single-city anchor for the SIOPE cash proxy; the waste tax cannot be separated, so there is no ex-TARI variant.
- Cesena study, `data/processed/cesena/indicatori_rendiconto.parquet`: long table (`comune_id`, `year`, `code`, `value`) of the synthetic indicators in the Piano degli indicatori, rendiconto 2020 onwards (2023 onwards from the portal, 2020-2022 from the old site; 2020 and 2021 print 35-36 of the 54 indicators) (code as printed, e.g. `1.1` structural rigidity in %, `4.4` personnel per head in euro). Needs `pdftotext` (poppler) to rebuild.
- Cesena study, `data/processed/cesena/conto_annuale.parquet`: one row per year 2020-2024 from the three-year summaries of the Conto annuale PDFs (`heads` at 31 December, `fte` from months paid, `pay_eur` fixed and accessory pay, `arrears_eur`, `labour_cost_eur` = Tab. 12+13+14, nominal euro); permanent staff of the municipality alone, the later file wins for a year in two files. Needs `pdftotext`. Three more tables from the same PDFs, same rules: `conto_annuale_flexible.parquet` (`kind`: fixed-term, agency, training contracts, co.co.co., consultancy, services; `units` = full-time equivalents or number of contracts, `cost_eur` Tab. 14; `fixed_term_heads` is the Tab. 2A total, without cost), `conto_annuale_levels.parquet` (mean pay per head by contract level: `fte`, `avg_total_eur`, `avg_fixed_eur`, `avg_accessory_eur`; `regime` `categorie` A-D up to 2022 and `aree` from 2023 after the CCNL 2019-21, not comparable level by level; the `ALTE SPECIALIZZAZIONI` rows are not parsed, so levels do not sum to the total) and `conto_annuale_funds.parquet` (`resources_eur` and `used_eur` of the decentralised fund, organisational positions, overtime, Elevata Qualificazione, managers' and secretary's funds; a blank cell is 0, and the positions fund `po` moves to `eq` in 2023, so compare their sum). A later file revises an earlier one (the 2023 file prints 65 fixed-term heads for 2023, the 2024 file 62, which is the sum of its categories).

## Sources by prefix
- `wgi_*`: World Bank Worldwide Governance Indicators, 0-100 score (2025 methodology, history recalculated; perception-based); `vat_compliance_gap_pct`: EU VAT gap report 2025 (Table 205, % of VAT due; years before 2013 flagged `backcast`, an assumption about where the source's backcast ends; Luxembourg excluded as unreliable in the source); `proc_*`: hand-entered from the Single Market Scoreboard.
- (no prefix): Eurostat; `wb_*`: World Bank (WDI, and EdStats for PISA); `imf_*`: IMF WEO (DataMapper; years > 2025 dropped); `ameco_*`: AMECO (EC DG ECFIN); `oecd_*`: OECD Pensions at a Glance.

## Known coverage limits
- Regional extras (notebook 34): EQI has 5 waves and only NUTS2 rows are used; `nuts2_sbs_*` end in 2020 and are missing where any of the 12 sections is confidential (Italy complete in about 87% of region-years); Istat migration (2002-2025) recent years provisional; CPT to 2023.
- Crime: police-recorded series reflect recording practices; compare trends and use homicide (`homicide_victims_per_100k`, `wb_homicide_rate`) for levels. Italy's serious-assault series breaks in 2013-2016 and is not included; Italy does not report total burglary, so `burglary_residential_rate_police` is used. Eurostat series start in 2008.
- Migration: registrations only; emigration is under-recorded in Italy and elsewhere. Net migration of nationals aged 25-34 is available for about 15-21 countries (not France, Portugal or Greece).
- `arope_linked` joins the old and new AROPE series using each country's mean new/old ratio over 2015-2020; values before 2015 are approximations (flag `linked`). `wb_gini` (World Bank, from EU-SILC) is on a different income concept from Eurostat's `gini` (Italy 2014: 34.7 vs 32.4) and is not spliced with it.
- `healthy_life_years_*` are self-reported (EU-SILC activity limitation) and show level shifts when the survey changed: check the series before quoting a rank.
- `treatable_mortality_rate` / `preventable_mortality_rate` end in 2023.
- Wages and gender: `net_earnings_pps` / `gross_earnings_pps` (average single worker, PPS) have a series break in 2024 (Eurostat flag `b`, `SERIES_BREAKS` in `catalog.py`): the average wage has a new basis (gross euro pay of the average worker: Italy +13%, Germany -7% on 2023), so 2024 onwards is not comparable with 2023 and before; never compare or average across it. Until 2026-11-10 the 2024 values were published on a monthly scale and were dropped at build time. `gender_overall_earnings_gap` is four-yearly and currently ends in 2018; `gender_pay_gap` latest points are provisional; `women_managers_share_pct` has a series break in 2011; `labour_cost_hourly_eur` is biennial before 2020. Italy has no statutory minimum wage.
- Eurostat covers the UK only partially and almost never US/JP -> use `imf_*`, `wb_*`, `ameco_*` for benchmark comparisons (definitions differ; do not mix with Eurostat series on one axis).
- `gini`: from 2014 only. `arope`: 2015+; `arope_old`: 2005-2020 (methodology break, never splice silently).
- `arop_65plus` is the 60%-of-median threshold (`ilc_li02`, statinfo MED_EI, rskpovth B_60).
- `wb_gov_debt_pct_gdp` is central-government debt, only a few countries: prefer `imf_gov_debt_pct_gdp` (general government) or Eurostat `gov_debt_pct_gdp`.
- `ameco_comp_per_employee_ncu` is in national currency (thousands): use only as index/growth. `ameco_tfp_index` is 2020=100: compare growth, not levels.
- PISA (`wb_pisa_*`) ends at the 2018 wave in World Bank EdStats; the 2022 wave comes from the OECD workbook via `scripts/import_pisa_2022.py` (Luxembourg did not take part; Spain's 2018 reading score is missing in the World Bank series). PIAAC (`piaac_*`, 2023 and derived 2012) comes from a browser export of the OECD Education GPS table (see `docs/manual_data.md`); 19 EU members take part (Belgium and the UK only as regions).
- `oecd_pension_exp_pct_gdp` stops in 2021 (25 countries).
- Latest years (2024/2025) may be provisional; check `flag` and `manifest.json`. IMF 2025 values are estimates.
- `labour_productivity_pps_eu27` is real labour productivity per hour as an index (2020=100): compare growth, not levels.

## High-frequency table
- `data/processed/highfreq.parquet`: monthly (`m_`) and quarterly (`q_`) Eurostat series for the EU27, GBR/USA/JPN where available, and aggregates: `iso3, geo, period` (`2023-01` or `2023-Q1`), `date` (period end), `freq` (`M`/`Q`), `indicator, value, flag, theme, source`. Seasonally (and calendar) adjusted where Eurostat offers it; sentiment balances are seasonally adjusted. Coverage 2005-01 (or 2005-Q1) to the latest release; 23-31 geos per series (quarterly employment 23, services confidence 27).
- HICP uses `prc_hicp_minr` (ECOICOP ver. 2, `coicop18`): the old `prc_hicp_manr` stops in December 2025. Italy's December 2022 inflation reads 12.2% here (12.3% in the old classification).
- Flags: `d` definition differs, `p` provisional, `e` estimated, `b` break, `u` low reliability (dropped by default in `hf.wide_hf`).
- External: `data/external/ecb_events.csv` (five ECB decisions with an evidence label per row).

## Daily yields and rating changes
- `data/processed/daily_yields.parquet`: daily 10-year government bond yields (percent) for Italy and Germany, 2005-01-03 to 2026-10-14, trading days only: `iso3, date, indicator` (`d_long_term_yield`), `value, source` (`manual:investing.com`). Built by `cifresignificative.core.sources.daily_yields` from the manual exports in `data/external/source/daily_yields/` (two files per country because the site caps an export at 5,000 rows; overlapping days must agree exactly). Weekend rows (Italy 370, Germany 182, mostly 2013-2023) are indicative quotes and are dropped. Monthly means track the Eurostat monthly convergence yield (correlation 0.9995 Italy, 0.9999 Germany). A commercial aggregator, not an official series; no other countries.
